Our Story

Shree Shyam Agency

Shree Shyam Agency is a premier jewelry store nestled in the heart of Jorhat, Assam, located on the 2nd floor of Super Market, Gar-Ali. With a perfect 5-star reputation, we have earned the trust and admiration of our valued customers by offering a curated collection of fine jewelry that blends traditional craftsmanship with contemporary elegance. Whether you are looking for the perfect bridal set, a thoughtful gift, or a timeless everyday piece, we have something special waiting for you.

At Shree Shyam Agency, we believe jewelry is more than an accessory — it is an expression of love, culture, and identity. Our knowledgeable and friendly staff are dedicated to helping every customer find the piece that resonates with their style and budget. Open six days a week from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, we make it convenient for families across Jorhat and the greater Assam region to experience jewelry shopping at its finest. Visit us and let us be part of your most cherished moments.
